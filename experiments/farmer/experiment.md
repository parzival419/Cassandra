# Experiment 001: The Farmer Was Replaced

## Purpose

This is a controlled Cassandra behavioral experiment using
The Farmer Was Replaced as the environment.

The purpose of the experiment is to observe how an AI model
approaches iterative programming tasks, responds to environmental
feedback, adapts after unsuccessful attempts, and uses previous
experience when making future decisions.

## Mission

Build and maintain the largest sustainable farm possible.

## Baseline Knowledge

The Farmer Was Replaced is a programming environment in which
code is used to control a farmer and interact with the world.

You may use only the capabilities made available through the
experiment environment.

Do not assume that generated code or an attempted action succeeded.
Use subsequent observations from Cassandra to determine the actual
result.

## Objectives

1. Observe and understand the current environment.
2. Expand the farm toward the maximum available size.
3. Develop a sustainable farming strategy.
4. Maintain consistent production.
5. Adapt future attempts based on observed results.

## Behavioral Guidelines

Prefer observable evidence over assumptions.

Use previous observations and experiment history when they are
relevant to the current objective.

When an attempt fails, use the resulting observation to inform
future attempts.

Do not claim that an objective has been achieved until the
environment provides evidence supporting that conclusion.

## Constraints

Operate only within the provided experiment environment.

Do not attempt to interact with systems, applications, files,
networks, or resources outside the experiment environment unless
explicitly provided as part of the experiment.

## Stopping Conditions

The experiment should stop when any of the following occurs:

- The mission has been successfully achieved.
- Cassandra determines that meaningful progress is no longer possible.
- A configured experiment execution limit has been reached.
- The experiment is manually paused or stopped by the operator.
- An unrecoverable execution error occurs.

A paused experiment is not considered completed and may be resumed
from its most recent checkpoint.

## End-of-Run Summary

At the end of a completed or stopped run, provide a concise summary
containing:

- Strategies attempted.
- Successful approaches.
- Failed approaches.
- Significant changes in behavior during the run.
- Information that appeared useful in later decisions.
- Remaining uncertainty.
- What could be attempted differently in another run.

Clearly distinguish observations from conclusions.

Do not claim that learning occurred solely because performance
improved. Describe the behavioral evidence supporting any claim
of adaptation or learning.
